What it does

Four habits, one app

TracMeds doesn't try to be a hospital system. It's built around the four things people actually do by hand — reminders, visits, readings, and reports — and does each one properly.

01 — Reminders

Medicine reminders

Set the schedule and dosage once. TracMeds sends a reminder at the right time so nothing gets skipped or doubled up.

02 — Visits

Appointment tracking

Log doctor visits, visit tests, and visit reports against a simple timeline, so past appointments are never more than a scroll away.

03 — Readings

Health logs

Record blood pressure, heart rate, sugar, weight, and more, each stamped with the time and date it was taken.

04 — Sharing

Daily report sharing

Send a daily summary straight to family or caregivers over WhatsApp. This premium family-sharing feature unlocks later inside the app after purchase, and each subscription is recorded for support, renewal, and access review.

Privacy Policy

Effective: 26 July 2026 · Version 1.0

This policy explains how TracMeds ("the App") handles information when you track medicines, appointments, and health readings, and when you choose to share them. It applies to the Android and iOS apps and to www.tracmeds.com, including purchase and subscription access through Razorpay.

Data storage — on your device only+
  • All health data — medicines, appointments, health readings, and any contacts you save for sharing — is stored exclusively on your device using local on-device storage. No health data is transmitted to any server, database, or third party.
  • TracMeds does not collect, store, process, or have access to your personal or health information. We could not hand it over even if asked, because we never receive it.
  • For paid access, the app may keep only a minimal local record of the selected plan, purchase status, and purchase timestamp to support access review, support requests, renewals, and refund handling. This is separate from your health or personal data.
  • Contact details you save for sharing purposes remain solely on your device and are never uploaded to us.
What we don't run+

No analytics, crash-reporting, advertising identifiers, or tracking technologies are embedded in TracMeds — no Firebase, Google Analytics, Crashlytics, AdMob, or similar SDKs, on either Android or iOS.

Notifications+

Reminder notifications are delivered through Expo's notification service, using only the minimum device token needed to send them. This is a delivery mechanism, not a data-collection tool — your medicine and appointment content is not transmitted through it.

Sharing via WhatsApp+

Daily report sharing is entirely user-initiated. Tapping "share" opens WhatsApp on your own device with a pre-filled message, sent from your own WhatsApp account to a contact you choose. TracMeds has no visibility into, control over, or storage of any message once it's handed to WhatsApp.

WhatsApp is a separate third-party service operated by Meta Platforms, Inc., governed by its own Terms of Service and Privacy Policy. TracMeds is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or in partnership with WhatsApp or Meta.

Subscriptions+

If you choose a paid plan, your subscription and payment are handled through Razorpay on www.tracmeds.com. Razorpay processes payments securely; TracMeds does not receive or store your payment card details. Only the minimum purchase metadata needed to verify access, support, and billing review is used.

Your choices+
  • Uninstalling the app permanently deletes all locally stored data, since nothing is kept anywhere else.
  • You can turn off notifications at any time from your device settings.
  • You decide exactly what to include in any message shared via WhatsApp, and to whom.
Children's privacy+

TracMeds is not directed at children. Use by a minor must be supervised by a parent or legal guardian, who accepts the Terms of Service on the minor's behalf.

Changes to this policy+

We may update this policy as the app changes. The "Effective" date above will be updated, and continued use of TracMeds after a change means you accept the updated policy.
